FAQ

Good to know

Sizing, fabrics, delivery and returns — the questions we hear most. Can't find yours? Ask the team.

How do I choose the right size?

Every product page lists chest, waist and hip measurements. If you are between sizes, go up for a relaxed fit or down for a compression feel.

What are Virex fabrics made of?

Our tops and bottoms use lightweight technical knits with four-way stretch, moisture wicking and a thermoregulating finish.

How should I wash my apparel?

Machine wash at 30°C with similar colours, no fabric softener, and air dry. This keeps the stretch and the reflective logo in shape.

How long does delivery take?

Orders ship within 24 hours. Delivery takes 1–3 working days in Ukraine and 4–9 working days across Europe.

Can I return an item?

Yes. Unworn items with tags can be returned within 30 days for a refund or exchange.
